# Evaluation gates

The repository foundation is complete when the declared files exist, references are coherent, draft schemas parse, and the bundle template is clearly non-activatable. That is documentation evidence only.

Future agent and harness gates need behavioral proof: a plain-language builder interview and correction loop; source/license decision; task acceptance; denied-action and prompt-injection cases; retry, timeout, cancellation, and recovery; bundle export/import; discovery and invocation in each advertised host; and consistent effective policy on at least two independent adapters. Record environment, version, fixture, expected result, actual result, and limitations. Do not generalize an isolated dry-run to live availability.

Network-agent evaluations will require vendor/model-specific lab or simulation evidence, a backup/recovery proof, exact-action approval, and independent post-change checks before any live reset or firmware operation.
