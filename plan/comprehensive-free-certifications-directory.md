## How to Add Any Credly Badge to LinkedIn with 1-Click

1. **Earn & Accept:** Pass the assessment on the provider portal and click the badge claim link sent to your registered email.
2. **Access Credly Dashboard:** Log into [Credly.com](https://www.credly.com) and navigate to your **Earned Badges**.
3. **Click Share:** Click on your new credential, then select the **Share** button in the upper right.
4. **Select LinkedIn:**
   - **Add to Profile:** Seamlessly opens LinkedIn with pre-populated Credential Name, Issuing Organization (e.g. *Amazon Web Services*, *Tigera*, *Cisco*, *Datadog*), Issue Date, and unique Verification URL.
   - **Share to Feed:** Generates a high-resolution, interactive verification card directly on your LinkedIn feed.
