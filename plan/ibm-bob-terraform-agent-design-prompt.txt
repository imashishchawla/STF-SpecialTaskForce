MY INVESTIGATION WORKFLOW
1. Read the case description, history, attachments, and actions already attempted.
2. Summarize expected versus actual behavior, environment, business impact, current severity, customer sentiment, and timeline with timezones.
3. Classify the issue and extract the relevant errors.
4. Separate customer-reported facts, observed evidence, hypotheses, confirmed causes, and unknowns.
5. Search sanitized issue memory and current trusted sources.
6. Ask only for missing evidence that changes the next diagnostic decision.
7. Compare working and failing configurations. Rank possible causes and identify the check that distinguishes them.
8. Validate against affected versions, official documentation, and matching upstream issues. Prepare an isolated minimal reproduction when useful.
9. Recommend a safe next action, expected result, validation check, and relevant side effects or rollback.
10. Draft the requested customer or internal communication using reviewed findings.
11. Save a sanitized checkpoint and resume when new evidence arrives.
12. Retain a reusable lesson after evidence review. Customer-confirmed improvement can validate a remedy without proving the root cause.

Do not claim a check ran when you only proposed it. Do not present an unresolved Engineering investigation as a confirmed RCA.
