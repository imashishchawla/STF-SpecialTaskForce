# Federal Political & Legislative Migration Radar

**Last Audit Timestamp:** 2026-11-10T02:13:05.076859+00:00  
**Macro Status:** Active Horizon Monitoring

## Current Federal Environment & Net Overseas Migration (NOM)
The Australian government has announced further migration reforms aimed at reducing Net Overseas Migration (NOM) to 245,000 by 2027 and 225,000 by 2028. The latest figures from the Australian Bureau of Statistics show that NOM has fallen to 292,000, down significantly from its post-pandemic peak of 556,000 in September 2023. The government’s own Treasury forecasts now project a slower return to pre-pandemic levels than previously anticipated, with NOM forecast at 295,000 in 2025-26 and 245,000 in 2026-27. The overall permanent migration program remains capped at 185,000 places, unchanged from previous years.

## Active Legislative Signals & Planning Ceilings
* **Federal Permanent Migration Cap:** Retained at 185,000 places with approximately 70% allocated to the Skill stream.
* **Temporary Visa Crackdowns:** Focus remains on student visa integrity, graduate visa age caps, and reducing onshore visa hopping. Enterprise tech PR streams remain insulated.
* **Housing vs. Immigration Political Debate:** Heightened media rhetoric leading into the federal electoral cycle, but legislative bipartisan consensus continues to prioritize skilled workers in core engineering and tech disciplines.
