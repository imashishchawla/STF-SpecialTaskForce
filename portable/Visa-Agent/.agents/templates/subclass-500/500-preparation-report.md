## Main findings and priority actions
Answer the applicant's main question first. State the most important confirmed preparation gaps or clearly labelled possible concerns, the evidence supporting them and the next concrete actions in priority order. If no problem is supported, say so within the stated review scope. Name material uncertainty and how to resolve it. Do not infer eligibility or approval. Keep the detailed evidence review below; omit irrelevant sections. This template is for broad reviews, not every short question.

## What I checked
State task, role, stage, relevant dates and actual documents/pages inspected. Explain extraction/browsing limits. If relevant, name the exact primary/family/status/package gate, the version applicable to the applicant's date and what was actually checked. The stored16November2026 targeted refresh includes LIN20/158 ss16–24 and successor-Regulations/later-amendment coverage; it is not a live case check or full-code certificate. Identify material CoE-date, course-definition, package or individual newborn/status questions that remain unresolved, rather than repeating that all delegated classes are unknown. Distinguish stored guidance from verified applicable compliance; do not discard a substantive exception merely because accessible corroboration is absent. This is a preparation check, not eligibility, authenticity or approval.

## Checklist and evidence review
| Item | Requirement type / trigger | Stage | Evidence reviewed | Status | Why / next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

For each assessed item distinguish presence, readability, completeness, consistency, currency and verified-rule compliance. Use separate rows or short notes so a readable document does not appear fully compliant by implication. Use the status words in SKILL.md. Unknown applicability/content means Needs verification. If only not provided, say not supplied for this review.

## Procedural records — only when relevant
Keep this section short or omit it. Distinguish application acknowledgement/date/timezone from payment receipt; residence from correspondence/recipient; request items/channel/deadline from extension requested/granted; original documents from translations/certification; unavailable evidence from proposed alternatives; and birth/change chronology from applicant inclusion. Use neutral IDs and no full identifiers. Report only inspected records and material unknowns.

## Missing or needing correction
Confirmed gaps first. Give exact evidence location, why it matters and concrete action. Separate possible concerns from confirmed problems.

## Questions still to resolve
Explain each material unknown, its effect and what evidence/official clarification or professional question will resolve it. Do not dump internal issue IDs or unrelated research history.

## Next steps
List actions in a useful order, with verified due date/timezone where applicable. Do not invent urgency, amounts or deadlines.

## Brief verification note
Summarise current versus unverified checks. Include official links/titles and checked dates for consequential requirements. Do not add citations to every sentence or reproduce a full source register. Keep enough detail to let the applicant check the requirement and ask for more.

## Outcome
State only the preparation checks supported and remaining gaps. No approval probability, overall eligibility opinion or readiness score. Do not mark the whole application Ready with mandatory unverified/missing checks.
