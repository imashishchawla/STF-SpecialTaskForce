# Credential verification

## When to use

Use to review a candidate record, update evidence, resolve duplicates, or prepare publication-safe Markdown.

## Steps

1. Read the candidate record and source policy. Verify identity and version against the issuer or exam owner.
2. Open each source directly. Match its authority to the claim. Do not treat search snippets or another agent's prose as evidence.
3. Verify cost by region/currency and distinguish free training from paid exam/certificate. Record access date separately from publication/update date.
4. Record assessment style, delivery platform, and proctoring as separate facts. Derive a primary display label from the controlled list in `credential-record.md`; use `Unknown` when evidence is absent.
5. Check Credly issuance on the official issuer/badge page. Check LinkedIn addability only through an official issuer or LinkedIn flow that demonstrates the behavior/ID. Record unknown/not found when no direct evidence supports yes or no.
6. Verify accreditation with the accreditor/register and state the exact scope. A vendor association or badge is not itself proof of accreditation.
7. Preserve conflicts, unknowns, stale claims, and inaccessible sources. Never turn missing evidence into a negative claim.
8. Search the Markdown corpus for name, issuer, aliases, skills, exam code, and version duplicates. Link superseded records rather than deleting their history.
9. Return a patch and review result. Do not self-approve, edit the public site, claim a candidate verified, or publish.
